# Celestial Cutaway: guide

## Quickstart

1. Open `index.html` in a browser. Double-clicking it is enough, with nothing to install.
2. Press **Randomize** (or Space) until something catches your eye.
3. Press **Export** (or Ctrl+S) to save it as a PNG.

The images are yours to use, commercially included. The rest of this page is optional, and **Help** in the app covers the same ground.

## Making a body

**Choose a kind.** Archetype picks one of the 14 kinds of body. Presets set several controls at once for a named look, and keep your seed so you can compare looks on the same body.

**Keep what you like.** The padlock beside a control tells Randomize to leave it alone, so you can re-roll everything else. Randomize settings can lock a whole section at once.

**Adjust it.** Structure, Colour and Detail hold the sliders. The picture redraws as you move them, and hovering over any control tells you what it does. Which controls you see depends on the body: a machine world has Operational status and Hull integrity, for example.

**Pick its traits.** Under Traits, tick a feature to force it on, or click × so that Randomize never picks it.

**Annotate it.** Labels & Info turns on the info card (three levels of detail), the layer names and the scale bar. The labels and the bar are drawn into exports of the body.

**Frame it.** Scroll over the picture to zoom, and drag to move around. Past about 10× the picture turns abstract, which is deliberate. Output sets the background (solid colour, gradient, nebula or transparent), the shape (16:9, 1:1, 4:3 or 3:2) and the resolution (720 to 2160 px tall). Exports use the framing you set.

## Exporting

The Export menu offers:

| Choice | What you get |
|---|---|
| Current settings | A PNG of what you see |
| Body + info card | A PNG of the picture with its card |
| Info card only | A PNG of the card |
| 1:1, body only | A square PNG of the body |
| Factsheet | The body's facts as a Markdown text file |
| Copy to clipboard | The current picture, ready to paste |
| Copy seed + settings as text | A string that brings this exact body back |

**To get a body back later,** paste that string into the Seed box. The same seed and settings always draw the same body. Your current settings are also remembered between visits in the same browser, and **Reset to defaults** in Randomize settings clears them.

## Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Space | Randomize |
| ← / → | Previous or next seed |
| L | Show or hide the layer labels |
| I | Show or hide the info card |
| 1–8 | Open or close a settings section |
| Ctrl+S | Export a PNG |
| Ctrl+C | Copy the render to the clipboard |
| ? | Show the key list |
| Esc | Close a window, or leave a text field |

## If something goes wrong

- **Blank or broken page:** keep the whole folder together, because `index.html` needs the `js/` and `lib/` folders beside it. If you downloaded a zip, unzip it first rather than opening the file from inside it.
- **A bug in your browser:** the tool was tested on Firefox. If another browser misbehaves, leave a comment on the itch.io page or send an email, and I'll try to fix it.
